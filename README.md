### Hi, I’m Marina 👋

I am a supply chain and procurement professional with over 10 years of experience
in international logistics, supplier coordination and operational analytics.

My background combines hands-on supply chain execution with data-driven analysis.
I use analytics not as an end in itself, but as a tool to support business decisions,
performance evaluation and strategic development.

This repository contains selected analytical and business cases where I applied
data analysis to:
- evaluate business and marketing performance,
- assess efficiency and value creation,
- translate data into clear, management-level insights and recommendations.

My analytical work complements my professional experience in logistics,
procurement-related tasks, inventory and performance monitoring.

Tools & technologies:
Python (pandas, numpy, matplotlib, seaborn, scipy),
SQL (PostgreSQL),
Excel, Power BI, Tableau,
Jupyter Notebook.
